# SCUDEM X 2025: It Is Raining Space Dust

Team 1038 received the **Outstanding Award** for its response to SCUDEM X 2025 Problem A. We developed a mathematical model to study how a cosmic-dust particle's initial size, speed, and entry angle affect its atmospheric entry and survival.

## Project

The model combines gravity, atmospheric drag, and particle ablation in a coupled system of differential equations. We solved the system numerically with RK45 and compared the effects of different particle sizes, velocities, and entry angles. The project also includes a parameter sweep and a visualization of atmospheric filtering.

**Tools:** Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, and Jupyter.
